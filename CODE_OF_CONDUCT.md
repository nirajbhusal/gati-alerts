# Code of Conduct

[नेपालीमा](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ne.md)

Gati relays disaster information. People may arrive here worried, grieving or in a hurry. We want every
contributor, reporter and reader to be treated with respect.

## Our standards

Please:

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors and people writing in a second language.
- Write in Nepali or English, whichever is easier for you.
- Disagree about facts and code, not about people.
- Accept correction openly. Corrections are a normal part of this project.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination on any ground, including caste, ethnicity, gender, religion,
  region, language, disability, age or sexual orientation.
- Knowingly posting false hazard reports, rumours or misleading edits.
- Publishing someone's private information (phone number, address, photos of identifiable victims) without consent.
- Presenting yourself or Gati as an official government warning authority.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere someone represents the
project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer privately through the email on the
[contact page](https://nirajbhusal.github.io/gati-alerts/contact/). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer
may remove content, warn, or block an account, in proportion to the harm.
