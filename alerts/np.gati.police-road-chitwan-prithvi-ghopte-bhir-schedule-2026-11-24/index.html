This is a timed closure; the road is expected to be open at other hours. The latest notice (to 6:00 PM Mangsir 7 / 23 Nov) no longer lists this closure, but police have not announced that it has ended, so Gati keeps it shown. Last official listing: notice to 7:00 AM Mangsir 7. Check with local police before travelling (100).
