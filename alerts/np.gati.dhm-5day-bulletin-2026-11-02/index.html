Today light rain or snow is possible at one or two places in the mountains and hills, including Lumbini Province; mist or fog at a few places in the Terai, valleys and gorges at night and early morning. DHM's 3-day forecast maps show light rain at one or two places in the Koshi, Bagmati and Gandaki hills and mountains on Kartik 17 and 18, mainly fair elsewhere. DHM's special bulletin on Cyclone 'Murasu' issued on the evening of Kartik 15 says no significant impact on Nepal's weather is expected (see separate notice).
