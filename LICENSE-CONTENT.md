## What this does not cover

- **Official notices relayed by Gati.** The wording, figures and maps of notices from DHM, NDRRMA, NEOC, Nepal

- **The Gati name and logo.** These identify this project. A fork should use its own name and logo so that people
  are not confused about who is relaying an alert. "Gati" is a working name pending a trademark check.
